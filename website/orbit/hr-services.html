Two kinds of hiring, done properly

Hiring a welder isn't hiring a CFO.

They need different sourcing, different screening and a completely different pace. We run them as two separate practices, because treating them the same is why most agencies are slow at one and careless at the other.

Blue-collar hiring

Operators · Technicians · Drivers · Warehouse · Housekeeping · Security · Field staff

Volume roles where the job is filled on availability, verification and turning up — and where a week's delay stops a line.

  • Bulk sourcing from local networks, ITIs and trade contacts
  • Documents verified up front — Aadhaar, PAN, bank, prior employment
  • Skill and fitness checks appropriate to the trade
  • Shift-ready onboarding — rosters and attendance set before day one
  • Replacement cover if a hire doesn't stay, on terms agreed up front

How it works

From a role to a person on the payroll.

The same four steps whichever track you're on. You see every candidate at every stage — no black box, no weekly status call needed.

Tell us the role

Headcount, skills, location, shift pattern and budget. One conversation, and we write the scorecard we'll be judged against.

We source & screen

We do the volume work — outreach, filtering, first interviews, document checks — and hand you only the people worth your time.

You choose

A shortlist with notes, scores and verified documents, tracked in your Orbit recruitment pipeline. You interview and decide.

They start ready

Offer, joining letter and onboarding handled. On day one they already exist in Orbit — profile, attendance, payroll, everything.

Why this is different

Most agencies hand you a CV and disappear.

We hand you an employee who is already in your HR system — because we built the HR system.

No re-typing anyone

The candidate we screened becomes the employee record. Nobody keys a new joiner into a spreadsheet, then into payroll, then gets a digit wrong.

Documents verified once

PAN, bank and prior employment are checked during hiring and stored against the employee — so payroll and compliance already have what they need.

You keep the relationship

Your workspace, your data, your people. It is built so that stopping our hiring service never means losing the records we helped you build.

Priced to be obvious

A percentage for white-collar, a flat fee per hire for blue-collar — quoted for your roles before we start, so there is no invoice you need a meeting to understand.

We stand behind our screening

Where a hire doesn't work out, we would rather find the next one than debate whose fault it was. The cover and the period are agreed with you before we start.

One team for both tracks

Factory floor and finance department under one contract — useful when you're opening a site and need forty operators and a plant manager at once.

Straight answers

The questions everyone asks first.

Do I have to use Orbit to use your hiring service?

No. We'll hire for you either way. But hires land fully set up — profile, documents, attendance and payroll — only if you're on Orbit, and that's most of the reason our onboarding is faster than an agency's.

How fast can you fill a role?

Blue-collar roles typically produce a shortlist within a week, often faster for trades we hire regularly. White-collar depends on seniority — usually two to four weeks to a shortlist you'd actually interview.

What happens if the hire doesn't work out?

We look for a replacement rather than argue about it. The cover, and how long it runs, are agreed with you before we start work and set out in your engagement terms — commonly around a month for blue-collar roles and longer for senior ones.

Do you charge for candidates I don't hire?

Our usual arrangement has no retainer and no per-CV fee — the fee is tied to a hire actually starting. What applies to your engagement is confirmed in writing before we begin.

Can you hire in more than one city?

Yes. Blue-collar sourcing is local by nature, so we work through networks in each location; white-collar hiring is run centrally and is remote-friendly.

Who employs the people you hire?

You do. We recruit on your behalf — they join your company, on your payroll, in your workspace. We're not a staffing intermediary.

Tell us who you need.

One conversation is usually enough to scope a role and put a fee in front of you. No obligation on either side until terms are agreed in writing.
