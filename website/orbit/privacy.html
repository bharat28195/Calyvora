Privacy Policy

2. Our role, and yours

This distinction decides who is responsible for what, so it comes before everything else.

When your company uses Orbit

Your employer decides what employee information goes into Orbit and what it is used for. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 your employer is the Data Fiduciary; we are the Data Processor acting on its instructions. In GDPR terms, your employer is the controller and we are the processor.

If you are an employee using Orbit, ask your employer's HR team first. We cannot change, disclose or delete your records on your own instruction, because they are not ours to act on — we will pass your request to your employer and support them in answering it.

When you deal with us directly

For our own website visitors, trial enquiries, customers and job applicants, we are the Data Fiduciary and this policy applies to us directly.

4. Why we process it

  • To provide the service your employer has subscribed to: recording attendance, calculating payslips, routing approvals, generating documents.
  • To keep accounts secure: authenticating sign-ins, issuing session tokens, sending password-reset codes, and detecting misuse.
  • To support you when you or your employer raises an issue.
  • To bill our customers and maintain our own financial records.
  • To meet legal obligations, including statutory records employers are required to keep.

What we never do: we do not sell personal data, we do not share it with advertisers, we do not use your employees' data to train artificial-intelligence models, and we do not use one customer's data to serve another.

6. Where it is stored

Your data is stored in Singapore. We chose the region for speed — it is close to Indian users — and it means data may be transferred outside India for storage and processing. Where personal data of people in the EU or UK is involved, transfers are made under appropriate safeguards. If your organisation requires data residency inside India, tell us before you subscribe; we can discuss it, and we will not pretend it is already the case when it is not.

7. How long we keep it

  • While your employer is a customer, for as long as they keep the record.
  • After a subscription ends, access is suspended but the data is retained for [[NN]] days so it can be restored or exported, and is then deleted on request or at the end of that window.
  • Trial enquiries not converted: [[NN]] months.
  • Billing and tax records: as long as Indian law requires us to keep them, regardless of the above.
  • Server logs: a short rolling window for diagnostics.

8. How we protect it

Specifics rather than reassurance, because the specifics are what a security reviewer wants:

  • Separation between customers is enforced by the database itself. Every tenant table carries a row-level security policy, so a query that has not identified a company returns nothing at all — even if the application above it has a bug.
  • Encryption in transit (TLS) for every connection, including the one between the application and the database.
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Nobody at Calyvora can read yours, which is why a reset issues a new one rather than telling you the old one.
  • Access inside Orbit is by role — payroll and salary data are not visible to ordinary members or managers unless your employer grants it.
  • Administrative access to production systems is limited to those who need it.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may ask to access a summary of your personal data and how it is processed, ask for correction, completion or updating, ask for erasure, nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated, and raise a grievance.

Where the GDPR applies, you additionally have rights to portability, restriction and objection.

How to exercise them: if the data is in your employer's workspace, contact your employer's HR team — they control it. Otherwise write to connect@calyvora.in and we will respond within [[NN]] days.

10. Children's data

Orbit is workplace software and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect a child's personal data. If you believe we have, tell us and we will delete it.

11. Cookies

Orbit sets only what it needs to work: a cookie that keeps you signed in, and a stored preference for whether you chose the light or dark theme. We run no advertising trackers and no third-party analytics that identify you. Blocking the session cookie will stop you being able to sign in.

12. Changes

When this policy changes we will update the date at the top, and for anything that materially affects how we handle your data we will tell customer administrators by email before it takes effect.
