One platform, every module

Everything your people team touches — under one roof.

Stop stitching together five tools and a dozen spreadsheets. Orbit brings the entire employee lifecycle into one connected system.

Attendance & shifts

Live check-in/out, a day-wise log for every employee, shift schedules and a clean team sheet — the way Keka and Zoho do it, minus the clutter.

Leave & time off

Balances, policies and holidays that just work. Employees request in a tap; managers approve from their inbox or phone.

Payroll that reads attendance

Salaries, loss-of-pay auto-calculated from real attendance, and branded payslips. HR runs the whole month in one click.

Regularizations

Forgot to punch in? Employees raise a fix-up request; the right manager approves and the day is marked present automatically.

Employee helpdesk

People raise HR tickets and track them to resolution. HR gets a tidy queue with threads, priorities and status — nothing slips.

Recruitment & onboarding

Track candidates through your pipeline and turn a hire into a fully onboarded employee with tasks, documents and a warm first day.

Performance

Goals, reviews and check-ins that keep growth on the calendar instead of in someone's forgotten to-do list.

Documents & letters

Generate offer letters, payslips and certificates from your own templates — with your company's name and branding on top.

Roles & permissions

Owner, Admin, HR, Manager, Employee — each sees exactly what they should. Set up your org's personas in minutes.

Currency, timezone & language

Run India in ₹ and the US in $, each in the right timezone — one workspace, applied everywhere automatically.

My Finances

Every employee sees how they're paid — bank details, PF, ESI and professional tax in one place. Salary and PAN stay private: their own only, never a colleague's.

Expenses & reimbursements

Staff claim what they spent, managers approve, finance sees what's pending and what's been paid — without a single email thread.

Ask it anything

"How many people are on leave next week?" The built-in assistant answers from your own data and cites where it looked.

Knowledge base & projects

Policies and handbooks people can actually find, plus projects, sprints and tasks — so work and the people doing it live in one system.

Analytics

Headcount, tenure, joiners, attrition risk and goal attainment — the numbers you get asked for in a board meeting, already worked out.
