Our story

It started with a hire we were proud of.

She was the right person. Screened properly, documents verified, references actually called. We handed her over to the client on a Friday feeling good about it.

By the following month she was a row in a spreadsheet. Her attendance lived in one file, her salary in another, and the documents we had already checked were being asked for a second time because nobody could find them. When payroll ran, somebody typed her days into a different system by hand — and got one of them wrong.

That is not a story about one hire. That is every hire, at almost every company we placed people into. We were doing careful work at the front, and watching it come apart the moment it left our hands.

So we built the part that was missing. Orbit began as the system we needed ourselves: one record per employee, from the offer letter to the final settlement, with attendance feeding payroll directly instead of through somebody's typing. Then clients started asking whether they could use it for the people we hadn't hired for them. That question is why Calyvora exists as a product company today.

We are still small, still early, and small enough that the person whose name is on this page is the person you will deal with. That is why you can ring a director instead of opening a ticket.

Straight answers

The questions people actually ask us.

Including the awkward ones. We would rather you asked them now than found out later.

You're new. What if you're not around in two years?
Fair question, and we won't pretend it away. Two things: the services business has clients and revenue of its own, so the product is not the only thing keeping the lights on — and your data is exportable on demand, in full, without asking us. If we ever went away you would leave with every record you put in.
Why would we pick you over Zoho or Keka?
Sometimes you shouldn't. They are good products with far more people behind them. Pick us if you want one company that both finds your people and runs them afterwards, or if you want the person who fixes the problem to be the person who answers the phone. If you need a large vendor with a procurement process to match, say so and we'll tell you.
Who actually looks after our data?
Each company's records are isolated at the database itself, not by a filter in the application that somebody has to remember to write. Salary, PAN and bank details are restricted from colleagues by design. We will walk your IT person through exactly how, on a call, without hand-waving.
What happens if we want to leave?
You export your data and go. There is no exit fee, no hostage period, and no "contact your account manager to discuss retention". A product that has to trap you isn't finished.
Who do we call when something breaks?
A director, on the number printed above. We are small enough that this is a real answer rather than a slogan — and honest enough to say that when we grow, it will have to change.
Is there anything Orbit doesn't do yet?
Yes, and we'll tell you which before you buy rather than after. Ask about anything specific on the call — a straight "not yet, here's when" costs us one deal; discovering it in month two costs us the relationship.
What will it cost?
The platform is priced per employee per month, and the published rates are on the pricing page — not hidden behind a form. Hiring is a stated fee per hire, quoted before we start work. Whatever we agree goes in writing before anything begins; nothing on this website is a quotation, and nothing gets invoiced that you haven't already seen.

Talk to whichever half you need.

Hiring, the platform, or both — it's the same short conversation, and it reaches a director rather than a queue.
