Why we built Orbit

A hiring business that got tired of the handover.

Before Orbit

Priority HR Services, hiring for clients

Vision

HR work that nobody has to chase.

An offer, a joining letter, a laptop returned, a final settlement — every one of them is somebody remembering. We want the remembering to be the software's job, so the people can work on the parts that need judgement.

Mission

Hire them, and run them, in one place.

  • Fill the role — on terms agreed before we start
  • Hand over an employee, not a CV: documents verified once
  • Run the month — attendance, leave, payroll — with nothing in between
  • Close it properly when someone leaves

Your data is yours

Each company's data is isolated at the database, not by a filter someone remembered to write. Leaving us never means leaving your records behind.

We own the screening

We would rather carry the risk of a hire not working out than argue about whose fault it was. Where that lands commercially, we agree up front.

Pricing you can read

A rate per employee for the platform, a stated fee per hire for the service — quoted before we start.

Salary stays private

Colleagues can look each other up in the directory. Pay, PAN and bank details are theirs alone — built that way, not configured that way.
