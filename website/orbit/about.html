Why we built Orbit

You can't run HR well on someone else's spreadsheet.

Priority HR Services placed people into companies that then tracked them in a spreadsheet, keyed them into a second system for payroll, and lost the documents we had already verified. We watched a good hire become an admin problem, over and over.

So we built the thing that was missing. Orbit is the HR platform we needed ourselves — which is why a hire we make arrives already in it: profile, documents, attendance and payroll, from the first day.

Making Calyvora the parent is the same idea, one level up. The services business keeps doing what it does for its clients. The product company can now sell to companies that will never need us to hire for them.

What we hold to

Four things we work to.

Short list on purpose — how we prefer to operate, and what we look for in the work we take on. How any of it applies to a particular engagement is set out in that engagement's terms.

Your data is yours

Every company's data is isolated at the database, not by a filter someone remembered to write. It is built so that leaving us never means leaving your records behind.

We own the screening

We would rather carry the risk of a hire not working out than argue about whose fault it was. Where that lands commercially is something we agree with each client up front.

Pricing you can read

A rate per employee for the platform and a stated fee per hire for the service — quoted before we start, so there is no invoice that needs a meeting to explain.

Salary stays private

Colleagues can look each other up in the directory. What someone earns, their PAN and their bank details are theirs alone — the product is built that way, not configured that way.

Talk to whichever half you need.

Hiring, the platform, or both — it's the same short conversation, and it reaches a director rather than a queue.
