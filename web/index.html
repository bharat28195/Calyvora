The four hidden taxes

Every company is drowning in software

A 500-person company pays for 20–40 SaaS tools glued together with brittle integrations and spreadsheets. No vendor solves this — because each one's incentive is to own a category and integrate reluctantly.

01

The integration tax

Companies spend more connecting tools than on the tools themselves. Data is copied, goes stale, and disagrees across systems.

02

The context tax

A tab-switching marathon. Your work tool doesn't know your goals, your customer, or your docs. Software has no memory of the organization.

03

The intelligence tax

AI bolted on as a sidebar per silo is a toy. Real leverage needs AI that reasons across HR, work, customers, and finance at once.

04

The governance tax

Twenty vendors means twenty security reviews, twenty audit trails, twenty admin consoles, twenty bills — and twenty places a breach can start.

Our thesis

Competitors sell you the organs.
We sell the nervous system — and every organ we add makes the whole animal smarter.

Calyvora collapses all four taxes to near-zero by making the entire operational surface of a company run on one Foundation: one identity, one data fabric, one event backbone, one permission model, and one AI layer. Integration isn't a feature — it's the substrate.

The OS-Apps

Nine applications. One operating system.

Each app is independently valuable and independently deployable — but none reimplements identity, permissions, search, files, events, audit, or AI. They all consume the shared Foundation. That's what makes them integrated by construction, not by integration project.

The Foundation Platform

The shared substrate every app runs on

The apps are the organs. This is the nervous system — the shared services no app reimplements, which is exactly why they're integrated for free.

Identity · Tenancy · AuthZ

One login, one org graph, one permission model — RBAC + ABAC, Zero-Trust, per-tenant keys.

Data Fabric · Search · Files

Every fact has exactly one System of Record. Apps share via events, never copies.

Event Backbone · Workflow

Immutable domain events decouple apps and give a replayable, auditable history by default.

AI Platform

RAG, agents, knowledge graph, and vectors as a first-class Foundation primitive — not a bolt-on.

Audit · Observability · Billing

One immutable audit trail across every app and every AI agent. One control plane.

Config · Secrets · Security

Secure-by-default primitives. The secure path is the easy path; the insecure path is loud.
